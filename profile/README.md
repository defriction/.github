## Who we are

We are a small, **AI-first software studio from Colombia**. Four developers, no layers: the
people who scope the problem are the people who ship it. We pair no-code speed with real
engineering to diagnose manual processes, automate the repetitive and build custom digital
products — and we run our own SaaS on the same stack we sell.

We work for teams that want to scale **without multiplying their operational load**:
turn manual work into systems that run 24/7, and keep the tools you already use instead of
replacing them.

## Stack

`Astro` · `React` · `Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Python / FastAPI` ·
`Node / NestJS` · `Go` · `PostgreSQL / Supabase (self-hosted)` · `Docker + Traefik` ·
`GitHub Actions` · `n8n` · `Telegram Bot API` · `WhatsApp Cloud API` · `Airtable` · `Stripe`

**Principles we don't negotiate:** self-hosted where it protects your data, TypeScript and
tests on anything that touches money, and automation with a human who signs the diff.
